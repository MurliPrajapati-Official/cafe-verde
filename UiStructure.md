Important Rule

Animation priority should be:

Food > Reviews > CTA > Decorations

Most AI-generated restaurant sites animate backgrounds, particles, and floating shapes.

Those add visual noise.

The things that should move the most are:

Food images
Review statistics
Order buttons
Direction buttons

because those are the elements directly tied to conversion.
